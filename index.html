FilmMate Support

FilmMate is an iPhone and iPad scouting and production-planning app for directors and cinematographers.

Contact support

Email arunkumar.connect.work@gmail.com for help, purchase questions, or privacy requests. Include your app version, device model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Avoid sending confidential production information or precise location details unless they are necessary to your request.

FilmMate Pro

FilmMate Pro unlocks advanced shot and production planning, PDF/CSV shot-list exports, iCloud plan collaboration, and sun, weather, and matching-shot tools. Open Settings → FilmMate Pro to subscribe or restore purchases. Quarterly and annual plans renew automatically. Final prices are shown by the App Store before purchase.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem service. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Permissions and hardware

Allow camera access for capture and AR, microphone access for voice memos, and location access when you want to use your current position. Calendar events and notifications are created only when you choose. You can change access in iOS Settings → Apps → FilmMate. LiDAR measurements and room scans need compatible LiDAR hardware.

Offline use and saved data

Local projects, locations, references, notes, and many planning tools work offline. Weather, new map imagery, geocoding, and iCloud collaboration require a network connection. Keep copies of important exports; captured and reference media are not included in cloud plan sharing. Removing the app can remove its local data.

Estimates

Framing, solar, lunar, and light-window calculations are estimates. Verify framing on the intended camera and check actual site conditions before a shoot.
