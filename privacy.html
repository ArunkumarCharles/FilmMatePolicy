FilmMate Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

This policy describes FilmMate for iPhone and iPad, developed by Arunkumar Charles. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to arunkumar.connect.work@gmail.com.

Local scouting data

Projects, locations, contacts you enter, permit and access notes, shot references, captured images, voice memos, markup, schedules, scripts, and camera/lens settings are stored on your device. FilmMate has no separate user account and operates no developer server for this data. FilmMate does not include third-party advertising or analytics, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal data.

Permissions

Camera access supports reference capture, the viewfinder, AR tools, set measurements, and room scanning. Microphone access is used for voice memos. Location access supports your current position, saved locations, capture metadata, and local sun information. You may also choose a location manually. Captured images and exports can contain coordinates and other production details. Calendar write access is used when you add an event; notification access is used for reminders you choose to schedule. Imported photos, videos, scripts, and files are selected by you through system pickers.

Apple online services

FilmMate uses Apple Maps, geocoding, WeatherKit, and iCloud CloudKit. Coordinates and selected places are sent to Apple as needed for map imagery, reverse geocoding, forecasts, and location-based requests. These services are governed by Apple's privacy policy and require the corresponding connection and account settings.

Optional cloud collaboration

When you choose to publish a project plan, FilmMate sends the plan to your iCloud account through CloudKit. Shared plan data includes project names and dates, location names, coordinates and time zones, shot labels and order, status, priorities, planned durations, shoot days, and schedule items. People you invite can access and edit that shared plan. Captured images, imported reference media, notes, voice recordings, markup, room models, contact details, and permits are excluded from cloud plan sharing in this release. Local changes do not automatically publish; use the sharing controls to publish updates. Local deletion or uninstalling FilmMate does not necessarily delete a previously published iCloud plan. Manage shared access and retained cloud data through your Apple/iCloud account and its available controls.

Home Screen widget

The app shares the active location's name, coordinates, and time zone with its own widget using an on-device App Group. The widget can display the location name on your Home Screen. Clearing the active location removes the app's shared widget snapshot.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription purchases and payment information. FilmMate uses verified App Store transactions and product identifiers to determine Pro access. FilmMate does not receive your payment-card details. Manage, cancel, or restore subscriptions through Apple and the in-app controls.

Exports and sharing

You decide when to export or share files and whom to share them with. Exports may include photos, camera positions, location coordinates, notes, contacts, and other production information, depending on the export. Recipients may retain copies. Review the contents before sharing confidential production material.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you delete it or remove the app, subject to your Apple device backup settings. Deleting local shots or locations removes associated media through the app's deletion controls. Exports stored elsewhere, recipients' copies, and published cloud plans have separate lifetimes. FilmMate does not hold a central copy that its developer can retrieve or delete. If you contact support, we retain your message and the information you provide as needed to respond and resolve your request.

Your choices

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings, choose manual locations, avoid online services, choose not to use cloud collaboration, clear the active widget location, and delete local records. Requests about support correspondence can be sent to the email above.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to FilmMate. The effective date on this page identifies the current version.
